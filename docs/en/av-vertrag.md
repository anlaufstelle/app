# Data processing agreement — data categories

This excerpt translates the data-category boundary of the [German agreement template](../../src/core/dsgvo_templates/av-vertrag.md); it is not a rendered agreement.

Person notes and case, episode and task details use type-bound standard fields. The shipped fields are encrypted, depending on field configuration; this does not encrypt the database or every free text. Pseudonyms, titles, outcome goal descriptions, milestones and reasons remain plaintext. Event notes follow their field configuration. Person pseudonyms and case/episode/task titles must be short, without real names or GDPR Art. 9 data; online guidance does not technically validate content.

The agreement also covers age groups, contact stages, event documentation, encrypted file attachments, GDPR Art. 9 data where recorded, user and access-log data including IP addresses, and backups. Pseudonymization is intended; the application cannot prevent names in text or attachments.
